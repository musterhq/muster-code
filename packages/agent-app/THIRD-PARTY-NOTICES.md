## Device helpers installed at runtime
Devices installs, on the user's computer and only after they turn it on, Expo Device Hub 0.15.3 (https://github.com/expo/expo-device-hub, MIT), which vendors serve-sim and serve-emu (Apache-2.0), and scrcpy-server v4.0 (https://github.com/Genymobile/scrcpy, Apache-2.0). None of them is bundled with Muster. Device frames are read at runtime from the user's own Xcode (DeviceKit chrome, through the helper) and Android SDK (emulator skins, Apache-2.0); none of those files is copied into Muster.
